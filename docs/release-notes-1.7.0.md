### 🎚 Equalizer for all Windows sound · Эквалайзер для всего звука
A 10-band curve you drag over a live spectrum, 17 presets and a separate profile for every output device (headphones, speakers, Bluetooth — switches automatically). Powered by the free Equalizer APO engine, installable with one click; your own APO settings stay untouched.
Кривая на 10 полос поверх живого спектра, 17 пресетов, свой профиль для наушников и колонок.

### 🌈 Music visualizers · Визуализаторы
13 styles: old-player classics (bars and wave, oscilloscope, LED meter, ambience, alchemy) and new ones (radial spectrum, tunnel, particles, spectrogram, neon waves, liquid drop, mirror). In the player with the current lyric line on top, as live bars in the notch, and in a full-screen window (F11, arrows switch styles, auto-switch, big lyrics). They react to the real sound with beat and BPM detection, and only listen while on screen.
13 стилей, от классики старых плееров до новых; в плеере, в чёлке и на весь экран.

### 🎵 Lyrics and player · Тексты и плеер
Lines light up exactly on time, a per-app offset is learned (Chrome, Spotify), apps without track time get the song start detected by sound, lyrics are found faster (NetEase and Kugou in parallel with LRCLIB). Sleep timer: 15/30/60 minutes or after this track.
Строки точно вовремя, сдвиг для приложения, таймер сна.

### 📱 Telegram bot — PC remote · Пульт ПК в Telegram
Button remote, status, top processes, disks, battery, Wi-Fi, IP, brightness, modes, equalizer, notes, to-dos, reminders, timer, clipboard, “find my laptop”, weather, air raid alerts, parcels. Behind separate permissions that are **off by default**: programs (`/apps`, `/kill`, `/open`), files (`/ls`, `/get`, files sent to the bot are saved on the PC), screenshots, lock/sleep/hibernate/restart/shutdown with confirmation, Windows console (`/cmd`, `/ps`). The bot obeys only your chat.
