## N-Island 2.4.1 — control the browser from your phone

*made in TG [@Ne1zvestny1i](https://t.me/Ne1zvestny1i) & Claude*

Update from the app: **Settings → About → Update** · Обновление прямо из приложения: **Настройки → О программе → Обновить**.

- **Links from the chat open on the PC:** send the bot a link (one or several, even without https://) and it opens in the PC browser right away, with buttons for incognito, a new window, favorites and the browser remote. Every link is checked for phishing first: a suspicious one (risk 50+) doesn't open by itself — you get a warning with “Open anyway” and “In incognito”. Links inside normal text get “Open on the PC” buttons. Can be turned off in the bot settings; needs “Control from the phone”.
- **Browser remote** (/browser): the active tab and its address, other windows, back/forward, reload, tabs (new, close, reopen, next, previous, by number), scrolling, zoom, full screen, video (pause and sound on YouTube), open a link or search, incognito. Keys go only to the browser window — if Windows doesn't give it focus, nothing is pressed.
- **Commands:** /browser, /tabs, /tab, /back, /forward, /reload, /scroll, /zoom, /web, /incognito, /links, /fav.
- **“🧭 Browser” widget** (92 in total): the same buttons, copy the address to the phone, switch windows, favorites and recent links.

Браузер с телефона: ссылки из чата сразу открываются на ПК (подозрительные — после подтверждения), пульт и виджет «🧭 Браузер», вкладки, инкогнито, избранное.
